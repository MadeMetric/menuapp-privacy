Menu App Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Menu App helps travelers read restaurant menus in their own language. This page explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. The app is run by an individual developer in Thailand, who is the data controller under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What we collect

  • An anonymous ID. Your phone gets a random ID the first time you agree to this policy. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Photos you upload. Menu photos and photos of a place's front, and the text our AI reads from them.
  • Places you add or fix. Their name, location and the time.
  • Your location, sometimes. Your phone's location is used on your phone to show places near you. It is saved on our servers only when you add a place or move one.
  • Notes for staff. Short notes you ask us to translate (like "no egg"). We don't keep the text; we only count how many you send, for a daily limit.
  • Reports. When you report a menu as outdated or a photo as bad.

We don't collect contacts, use advertising IDs, track you across other apps, or sell any data.

Why

  • To read, translate and show menus, and to show places on the map (performing the service you ask for).
  • To keep the app working and fair: daily limits, spotting duplicate or bad photos, and preventing abuse (legitimate interest).
  • Sending your photos and notes to the AI services below, and storing data outside Thailand, happens with your consent, which you give on the welcome screen.

What's public

Menu photos, storefront photos, places and translated menus are shared with everyone who uses the app, and stay in the app for other travelers even if you later delete your data. Anyone with a photo's web address can view it. Photos are resized before upload, which removes hidden details such as where they were taken. Please photograph menus and signs, not people.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase stores the database and photos.
  • Google Gemini reads menu and sign photos and translates text. We use Gemini's paid service, under which Google does not use this data to improve its products.
  • Google Maps Platform shows the map and finds places near you.
  • Apple delivers the app and may provide crash reports if you allow them in your iPhone settings.

These companies store data on servers outside Thailand, including in the United States. We use them under their standard data protection terms.

How long we keep it

  • Menus, places and their photos stay while they're useful to travelers, or until removed.
  • Daily-limit counters are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Everything personal is deleted when you use "Delete my data".

Your rights and deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my data. This deletes the locations saved when you moved places, your counters and your anonymous ID. The menus, photos and places you shared stay for other travelers, with nothing linking them to you. If a particular photo should be removed, for example because it shows you or someone else, email us and we'll take it down.

You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data, object to how it's used, or withdraw consent, by emailing us (below). You may complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee.

Age

The app is for people aged 18 and over.

Removing content

If a photo shows you, or content you own, email us and we'll remove it quickly.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, the app will ask you to agree again.
